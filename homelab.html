The homelab

Everything here runs at home and stays private, reachable only over my Tailscale network.

Storage & services

A Synology NAS is the center of it all. It hosts:

  • Nextcloud

    Files, notes and whiteboards.

  • Home Assistant

    Home automation.

  • Mealie

    Recipes and meal planning.

  • Open WebUI

    A chat front end for local AI.

  • Excalidraw

    Sketching and diagrams.

  • Forgejo

    My own git server, the master copy of my sites and apps.

  • App repos

    A private F-Droid repo and a desktop app download page for my own apps.

Local AI: Gufo

Gufo runs Qwen language models on an ASUS ROG Flow Z13 with AMD Strix Halo. Services on the NAS use it instead of a cloud API.

Networking

Tailscale ties the machines, phone and headset together without exposing anything to the open internet.

Around the house

  • VR

    Meta Quest 3 and a KatVR treadmill.

  • Kiosk dashboard

    A Kindle Fire running a custom dashboard.

  • Android wrappers

    Small launcher apps I built for my self-hosted services.
