> Prices are typical prices as of Oct 2026, check before booking. Drive times are map estimates from central Lexington, KY. Gas math: 20 mpg at $3.97/gal.

Buses leave from the Lexington Greyhound station, 477 W New Circle Rd. A bike must be packed in a sturdy case and booked ahead as a bulky item ($21.99–$44.99 each way).

## Cincinnati, Winton Woods
- **Bus**: about 1:10 AM or 2:10 AM, about 1.5 hours, from $18.48. One return a day, about 1:55 PM.
- **Ride**: about 6.6 mi on city streets.
- **Hammock**: allowed. Trees must be at least 12 in wide, use straps or padding, hang no more than 4 ft up. [Rules](https://www.greatparks.org/about/policies/slackline-hammock-guidelines)
- You arrive in the dark, so wait at the station until daylight.

## Dayton, Taylorsville MetroPark
- **Bus**: about 3.5 hours, from $26.48. Return about 12:05 PM.
- **Ride**: about 10.5 mi, mostly on the paved river trail. Bike-in campsite, 2-night max. [Campsites](https://www.metroparks.org/rentals-permits/reserve-a-campsite/)
- **Hammock**: not clearly allowed. Call first.

## Knoxville, Camp Quarry Trails
- **Bus**: 3:45 PM, about 4 hours, from $30.48. Return 10 PM.
- **Ride**: about 3.9 mi. Sites have a hammock hang. [Camp](https://www.campquarrytrails.com/camp-sites-and-amenities)

## Lake Cumberland: not by bus
- The closest stop is London, KY. The bus leaves once a day at 3:45 PM, takes about 1h40, and costs from $22.48. [Route](https://www.greyhound.com/bus-routes/lexington-ky-london-ky)
- From London it's still about 41 mi to Burnside Island, so you'd need a ride. RTEC rural transit is about $1 a mile and must be booked ahead (unverified). Rideshare in London is unconfirmed.
- **Hammocks at the lake**: KY State Parks ban driving anything into trees ([rules](https://parks.ky.gov/info/things-to-know/campground-regulations)), so use straps. Corps of Engineers rules allow portable gear that isn't nailed into trees.
- **Daniel Boone National Forest**: free dispersed camping, but stay 300 ft from the lake and from roads leading to it. [Rules](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/danielboone/recreation/camping-cabins)
