> Prices are typical prices as of Oct 2026, check before booking. Drive times are map estimates from central Lexington, KY. Gas math: 20 mpg at $3.97/gal.

## Late fall 2026
- **Red River Gorge plus Natural Bridge camping**: peak color is usually Oct 15–31. About $73. [Leaf timing](https://redrivergorgetourism.com/when-is-the-best-time-to-see-fall-leaves-in-the-red-river-gorge/)
- **Smokies late color plus Dollywood Christmas** (starts Nov 6): about $570 for 2 nights.

## Winter 2026-27
- **Cincinnati Zoo Festival of Lights**: about $72 (ticket $28, parking $12). [Info](https://cincinnatizoo.org/events/festival-of-lights/)
- **Southern Lights in Lexington**: $35 a carload. [Info](https://www.khpfoundation.org/southern-lights/)
- **Perfect North Slopes ski day**: $89 lift ticket. [Tickets](https://perfectnorth.com/lift-tickets/)
- **Ober Gatlinburg**: weekday lift ticket about $64 (prior-season rate). [Prices](https://www.onthesnow.com/tennessee/ober-gatlinburg-ski-resort/lift-tickets)

## Spring 2027
- **Smokies wildflowers**: usually mid- to late April.
- **Dollywood Flower & Food Festival**: May 7 – June 20.

## Summer 2027
- **Lake Cumberland camp and kayak**: about $140. See [Lake Cumberland](/b/lake-cumberland/).
- **Land Between the Lakes**: $12 sites at Hillman Ferry.
- **Synchronous fireflies**: by lottery only. [NPS](https://www.nps.gov/grsm/learn/nature/fireflies.htm)
- **Kings Island on a weekday**: about $132.

## Fall 2027
- **Riverfest/WEBN fireworks, Cincinnati**: Sun Sep 5, free.
- **Kentucky Bourbon Festival, Bardstown**: Sep 16–19.
- **Red River Gorge peak color**: late October.

## Holidays 2027
- **Dollywood Smoky Mountain Christmas**: Nov 5, 2027 – Jan 2, 2028. [Info](https://www.dollywood.com/themepark/festivals/smoky-mountain-christmas/)
- **Great Wolf Lodge Mason, 1 night**: about $240.
