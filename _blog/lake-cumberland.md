## Where to camp
- **General Burnside Island State Park**: 74 electric sites and 9 tent sites, a free 6-lane boat ramp. Open Mar 13 – Nov 15, 2026. About $22–$36 a night (unverified). Weekend stays need 2 nights. [Book](https://kentuckystateparks.reserveamerica.com/camping/general-burnside-state-park/r/campgroundDetails.do?contractCode=KY&parkId=91811)
- **Waitsboro (Corps of Engineers)**: 8 waterfront electric sites, 5 tent sites. Open about May–mid-Oct (2025 dates). [Book](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/232733)
- **Cumberland Point (Corps)**: 14 waterfront sites. Open about May–mid-Sep. About $22–$50 (unverified). [Book](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/232563)
- **Kendall (Corps)**: about 115 sites, below Wolf Creek Dam, with a trout stream. Open about Apr–early Nov. [Book](https://www.recreation.gov/camping/campgrounds/232615)
- **Lake Cumberland State Resort Park**: a year-round campground, plus a lodge (about $89–$140, unverified) and cottages. [Book](https://kentuckystateparks.reserveamerica.com/camping/lake-cumberland-state-resort-park/r/campgroundDetails.do?contractCode=KY&parkId=91911)

## Boats
- **Conley Bottom** pontoon: $185 a day for 4 people, up to $415 for 12 (2026 peak). [Prices](https://conleybottom.com/watercraft-rentals/pontoon-boats/)
- **Lake Cumberland Pontoon Rental**: kayak $35 a day; pontoon $475–$575 a day. [Prices](https://lakecumberlandpontoonrental.com/services/)
- **Lee's Ford**: 4-hour minimum, fuel extra. [Rentals](https://leesfordmarina.com/rent-a-boat/)
- **State Dock houseboats**: from $2,459. [Compare](https://statedock.com/houseboating-101/compare-boats/)
- Boat fuel is usually extra. I found no posted fuel prices.

## Things to do
- Waterfalls you reach by boat: Seventy Six Falls, Mystery Falls and Sid Bell Falls. [Map](https://www.theheartoflakecumberland.com/map-of-lake-cumberland)
- Cumberland Falls is a separate park, not reachable by boat. It's about 26 mi from Burnside.
- KY fishing license: $7.40 for 1 day, $24.31 a year, plus $10.57 for a trout permit. [Fees](https://fw.ky.gov/Licenses/pages/fees.aspx)
- Folding bike: there's a short paved trail at [Rocky Hollow Park](https://www.cityofsomerset.com/park/rocky-hollow-park/) in Somerset. Otherwise, ride park roads.
- Groceries: there are Kroger and Walmart stores in Somerset.

## When to go
- Early June, or after Labor Day. Midweek is calmer, and July 4 is the busiest time.
- The lake is drawn down about 23 ft in winter.

## Sample midweek trips (1 person)
- **Budget**: 3 nights at Burnside Island, plus a kayak day and a fishing day. About $185 plus food.
- **Comfort**: 2 lodge nights and a pontoon day. About $405–$505 plus boat fuel.
- **5-day version**: Kendall campground, trout fishing and Cumberland Falls. About $300–$475.
