> Prices are typical prices as of Oct 2026, check before booking. Drive times are map estimates from central Lexington, KY. Gas math: 20 mpg at $3.97/gal.

## By bus (no car)
- **Cincinnati, Loveland**: ride the paved Little Miami trail and stay at a free permit campsite in Loveland. The bus is from about $19. About $100 total. [Campsite](https://www.lovelandoh.gov/261/Buckeye-Trail-Town)
- **Dayton**: about 100 miles of paved river trails, with $5 tent camping at Narrows Reserve. About $70. [Narrows](https://www.gcparkstrails.com/parks/narrows-reserve/)
- **Knoxville**: river greenways, camping at Camp Quarry Trails. About $140. [Camp](https://www.campquarrytrails.com/camp-sites-and-amenities)
- **Louisville**: the Louisville Loop and the Big Four Bridge, by Miller bus. About $155. [Loop](https://louisvilleky.gov/government/louisville-loop/ohio-river-valley)

## By plane
- **Sarasota**: the Legacy Trail, with camping at Oscar Scherer State Park for $26 a night. [Fees](https://www.floridastateparks.org/parks-and-trails/oscar-scherer-state-park/hours-fees)
- **St. Pete**: the Pinellas Trail and Fort De Soto camping. [Camping](https://pinellas.gov/camping-information/)
- **Washington DC**: the Mount Vernon Trail and free C&O Canal hiker-biker camps. [C&O](https://www.nps.gov/choh/planyourvisit/primitive-campsites.htm)
- **Chicago**: the Lakefront Trail and a hostel from $34. [Hostel](https://www.hichicago.org/reservations)

## Drive and camp
- **Greenbrier River Trail, WV**: 77 flat miles with free trailside camps, about 289 mi away. The best ride on this list. [Plan](https://www.greenbrierrivertrail.com/plan-your-visit/)
- **Beech Fork State Park, WV, and the Huntington PATH trail**: about 136 mi. [Park](https://wvstateparks.com/parks/beech-fork-state-park/)
- **Natural Bridge** (57 mi), **Carter Caves** (92 mi) and **Dale Hollow** (128 mi): state park camping. Riding is on park roads only.
- **Hillman Ferry, Land Between the Lakes**: $12 basic sites. [Campground](https://www.landbetweenthelakes.us/visit/hillman-ferry-campground/)

## Bike rules
- **Greyhound**: the bike must be packed in a sturdy case. It's a paid bulky item. [Baggage](https://www.greyhound.com/travel-info/baggage)
- **Amtrak**: folding bikes ride free as a carry-on. [Rules](https://www.amtrak.com/bring-your-bicycle-onboard)
- **Airlines**: a bagged bike counts as a normal checked bag, about $45–$50 on American, United and Delta.
