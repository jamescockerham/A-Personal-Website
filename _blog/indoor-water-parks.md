## Great Wolf Lodge, Mason OH
- About 106 mi, about 2 hours.
- Cheapest night about $194–$199.
- 3 nights about $639 with gas.
- [Site](https://www.greatwolf.com/mason) · [Prices](https://www.hotelscombined.com/Hotel/Great_Wolf_Lodge_Mason.htm)

## Wilderness at the Smokies, Sevierville TN
- About 199 mi, about 3h50.
- Weekday rooms from $184, including the resort fee.
- 3 nights about $631.
- Pairs well with Dollywood.
- [Site](https://www.wildernessatthesmokies.com/)

## Kalahari, Sandusky OH
- About 293 mi, about 6h20.
- About $189 a night before tax (unverified).
- 3 nights about $683.
- [Rooms](https://www.kalahariresorts.com/ohio/accommodations/)

- Midweek nights are usually cheaper.
- Check-in at Great Wolf is 4 PM.
