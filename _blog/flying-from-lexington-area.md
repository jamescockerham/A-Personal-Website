## The four airports
- **LEX (Lexington)**: closest. The Lextran bus is $1. Fares are often higher.
- **CVG (Cincinnati)**: about 80 mi. Usually much cheaper and has the most nonstops. Off-airport parking about $5 a day.
- **SDF (Louisville)**: about 78 mi. Allegiant and Breeze deals. Express lot $9 a day, or $6 a day booked online. [Parking](https://www.sdfpark.com/)
- **HTS (Huntington WV)**: about 125 mi. A few Allegiant Florida routes.
